# Clinic Management System — Project Requirements & Planning (Version 2.5)

## Project Overview

A web-based clinic/hospital management system for internal staff use only.

The system is designed initially for a small clinic with:
- Around 10–20 employees
- Future scalability planned
- Internal centralized database
- Role-based access control
- Medical record management
- X-ray upload support
- Operation room support

Patients DO NOT have access to the web application in the first version.

---

# User Roles

## 1. Super Admin
**Permissions:**
- Full system access
- Manage all users (Create/Edit/Delete Admins, Doctors, Nurses, Front Desk, X-ray, Lab staff)
- **Credential Provisioning:** Sole authority to auto-generate or manually set/reset passwords for all staff accounts. (Users cannot set their own passwords).
- View all records and reports
- Configure system settings and manage permissions
- Monitor backups and system-wide audit activity logs
- **Data Recovery:** Sole authority to view, audit, and restore soft-deleted records across the system.

## 2. Admin
**Permissions:** Manage clinic operations, schedules, appointments, and staff accounts (excluding Super Admins). Access operational reports. **Staff Scheduling & Availability Roster:** Admins are responsible for maintaining the daily shift roster for Doctors and Nurses. The system must track who is currently "on shift."
**Restrictions:** Cannot manage Super Admin accounts, access critical system configuration, set/reset passwords, or recover soft-deleted data.

## 3. Doctor
**Permissions:** Access assigned patient records, add medical notes/diagnoses (utilizing standardized ICD-10 coding), create prescriptions, view high-resolution X-ray results, request lab/X-ray operations, access schedules, and generate patient reports.
**Restrictions:** Cannot access unrelated patients or manage users.

## 4. Nurse
**Permissions:** Access assigned patient information, record vitals, add nursing notes, assist doctors, and view schedules. Access the **Triage Queue** to record pre-examination vitals before patients see the Doctor.
**Restrictions:** Limited medical editing permissions. Cannot manage users. Access to patient data is restricted by specific assignment.

## 5. Front Desk
**Permissions:** Register patients, assign unique Medical Record Numbers (MRN), create appointments, edit basic patient info, manage check-ins, and search patient records. Route checked-in patients to the "Waiting Room" queue. **Active Routing:** The Front Desk can only assign and route checked-in patients to Doctors who are currently marked as "Available/On Shift" in the daily roster.
**Restrictions:** No access to medical diagnoses or sensitive medical modifications.
**UX Requirement:** Module must be optimized for **keyboard-first navigation** to ensure rapid patient registration and processing without reliance on mouse clicks.

## 6. X-ray Doctor / X-ray Staff
**Permissions:** Upload X-ray images, view assigned patient data, add X-ray reports.
**Restrictions:** Access limited to imaging-related information.

## 7. Laboratory Staff
**Permissions:** Manage laboratory tests, enter results, upload lab reports, and access assigned patient data relevant to testing.
**Restrictions:** Cannot modify diagnoses, prescribe medication, or access unrelated modules.

# Patient Access & Identification
**Identification:** Every registered patient is assigned a unique Medical Record Number (MRN) to prevent duplicate records and ensure accurate record retrieval.
**Current Version:** Patients DO NOT have website access, accounts, or login capabilities.
**Future Expansion:** Patient portal, mobile app, appointment booking, report viewing.

---

# Clinical Workflow & Patient Safety
- **Triage Workflow:** Upon Front Desk registration and MRN assignment, patients are routed to a "Waiting Room" queue. The Nurse Triage view is used to record initial vitals before moving the patient to the Doctor's "Ready" queue.
- **Allergies & Critical Alerts:** Patient profiles feature a universally standardized, highly visible section for allergies and medical contraindications. This is permanently pinned to the top of the Doctor and Nurse views.
- **Structured Prescriptions:** Free-text prescription fields are strictly replaced with a structured data model including: *Medication Name, Dosage, Frequency, Route (e.g., oral), and Duration*.
- **Appointment Lifecycle (State Machine):** To ensure clear tracking across the Front Desk, Nurses, and Doctors, every appointment follows a strict status lifecycle:
  - *Scheduled:* Appointment created, patient not yet in the clinic.
  - *Checked-In:* Patient has arrived and been processed by the Front Desk.
  - *In Triage:* Patient is currently with the Nurse for initial vitals.
  - *Ready for Doctor:* Vitals recorded; patient is in the waiting queue for the Doctor.
  - *In Consultation:* Patient is currently in the room with the Doctor.
  - *Awaiting Diagnostics:* Doctor has requested Lab or X-ray; patient is routed to the respective department.
  - *Pending Payment:* Consultation/Diagnostics complete; waiting for cash settlement at the Front Desk.
  - *Completed:* All clinical and financial steps are finished, and the patient has left.
  - *Cancelled:* Appointment voided (requires a reason code).
- **Diagnostic Request Loop (Lab & X-Ray):** Centralized queue and automated system alerts for diagnostics to maintain a smooth workflow:
  - *Order Generation:* The Doctor submits a structured Lab or X-ray request directly from the patient's file.
  - *Pending Queues:* The request instantly populates on a dedicated "Pending Orders" dashboard visible only to the Laboratory or X-ray staff.
  - *Staff Alert:* A targeted system notification alerts the respective department of the new order.
  - *Fulfillment:* The Lab/X-ray staff perform the procedure, upload the results/images, and mark the order as "Completed."
  - *Doctor Alert:* A system notification is instantly pushed to the Doctor indicating that the patient's results are ready for review.
- **Hardware Integration & Barcoding:** - **Front Desk & Lab Lookups:** Support for standard USB Barcode/QR scanners (configured as keyboard-wedge devices) to instantly pull up patient records via their MRN, eliminating manual search delays.
  - **Lab Sample Tracking:** The Laboratory module must support generating and printing standard barcode labels for blood vials and specimen containers immediately upon receiving a Doctor's diagnostic request.

# Authentication & Security
- **Login System:** Basic Username/Password authentication only for the initial release. Self-service password creation/resetting is disabled; all credentials are strictly generated and distributed by the Super Admin.
- **Session Management:** **Auto-Logout / Session Timeout** feature that locks user accounts after a set period of inactivity (e.g., 10 minutes) to secure abandoned workstations.
- **Access Control:** Strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- **Data Encryption:** - **In Transit:** All internal network traffic routed over HTTPS/TLS.
  - **At Rest:** Database server and physical drives storing X-ray images utilize encryption (e.g., BitLocker, LUKS).
- **Audit Trail:** Logs all CRUD (Create, Read, Update, Delete) operations on medical records and sensitive data. Logs capture: Timestamp, User ID, Action Performed, IP Address, and the **"Before" and "After" states** of any modified data.

---

# Accounting & Billing
- **Payment Model:** Primarily Cash-based transactions handled by the Front Desk.
- **Receipts:** Immediate receipt generation and direct printing capabilities upon receiving cash.
- **Future Scalability:** System architecture extensible to support payment gateways, POS, ATMs, and insurance integrations.

---

# Database
- **Type:** Single centralized internal database (PostgreSQL).
- **Clean Slate Initialization:** As a new clinic implementation, the database will be initialized empty (seed data for Super Admin only). No legacy ETL (Extract, Transform, Load) or historical patient data migration scripts are required for this release.
- **Medical Standardization:** Enforced use of standardized medical codes (e.g., ICD-10) for diagnoses.
- **Data Integrity (Soft Deletes):** Records utilize "Soft Deletes" (flagged as inactive/archived rather than permanently erased) to maintain relational history. *Only the Super Admin has permissions to view or restore soft-deleted data.*
- **Stored Data:** Patient records, staff accounts, medical history, appointments, imaging, operation records, audit logs.

---

# Backup System
- **Strategy:** Implementation of the **3-2-1 Backup Rule**: 3 copies of data, stored on 2 different media types, with 1 copy off-site (e.g., encrypted portable drive rotated weekly, or lightweight automated cloud backup strictly for text-based data excluding heavy X-rays).
- **Requirements:** Automatic monitoring, verification, and recovery procedures.

---

# X-ray System
- **Features:** - Upload X-ray images (with strict definitions for supported file types and maximum file sizes).
  - High-resolution image viewer allowing doctors to clearly examine details.
  - Link images directly to patient records and attach imaging reports.
- **Future:** DICOM support, PACS integration.

---

# Operation Room Module
- **Features:** - Operation scheduling and room management.
  - Procedure tracking and staff assignment.
  - **Documentation:** Upload specific medical documents and attachments directly to the operation record.
  *(Note: Pharmacy and detailed inventory tracking are handled by a separate external application).*

---

# Reporting System
**Required Reports:**
- Appointment, patient visit, and doctor activity reports.
- X-ray and Laboratory reports.
- **Financial Reports:** Revenue tracking and daily cash receipts.
- **Operations Reports:** Room and Operation Room occupancy rates.
- **Performance Metrics:** Appointment cancellation rates and average patient wait times.

**Filters & Export:**
- Filter by date range, doctor, department, or patient.
- Export to PDF and Excel.

---

# System Notifications
- **Doctor Alerts:** Targeted push/system alerts for doctors (e.g., "Patient has arrived", "Lab results are ready").
- *(Note: Internal staff chat/messaging is strictly EXCLUDED).*

---

# Recommended Technical Stack
**Architecture:** Monolithic MVP designed with modular domains for future microservice extraction.
- **Backend:** FastAPI (Python)
- **Frontend:** React (Optimized for **desktop monitors** and **keyboard-first navigation**)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Storage:** Local disk storage initially (Consider S3-compatible service like MinIO).

---

# Important Design Principles
Modular, Scalable, Maintainable, Secure, Role-based, Expandable, Easy to use, and Fast internally.
